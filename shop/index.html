Frequently asked questions

What products does Tigris Lifesciences offer?

Tigris markets prescription medicines across therapy areas such as antibiotics, ENT, dermatology, gastroenterology, CNS and pain management, plus a nutraceutical range for bone and joint health, immunity, multivitamins, Vitamin D, iron support and general nutrition.

Can I buy every product online?

No. Only our wellness and nutraceutical products are sold through our online shop. Prescription medicines are supplied through doctors, pharmacies and distributors; contact us for product or distribution enquiries.

Are Tigris products made to quality standards?

Yes. Our products are made by trusted manufacturing partners in WHO and ISO certified plants and are tested on industry quality parameters.

Do I need a prescription for the nutraceuticals?

Most wellness products do not need a prescription. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take other medication, speak to a healthcare professional first. High-strength Vitamin D3 (60,000 IU) should be used only as advised.

How do I choose the right supplement?

Browse by health concern in our online shop, read the label and composition, and ask your doctor or pharmacist if you are unsure.

How can distributors and doctors reach you?

Call +91 98151 56254, WhatsApp +91 98788 34047 or email info@tigrislifesciences.in. You can also use the enquiry form on our Contact page.

TIGRIS is a spirited organisation led by Mr. Tejpal Singh, with over 35 years in the pharmaceutical industry, bringing essential medicines and nutraceuticals to market since 2010.
