About us

We think ahead

Healthcare with a difference

‘Healthcare with a difference’ is the prime focus of TIGRIS. We believe that health and happiness run hand in hand, and so do physical and emotional wellbeing. TIGRIS is a spirited organisation handled by the experienced Mr. Tejpal Singh. With over 35 years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry, he seeks to fill the crucial industry gap.

Tigris was launched to seek, identify and introduce to the market essential drugs in areas of Speciality Primary Care, Respiratory, ENT, Dermatology, Gynaecology, IVF, Diabetology and Metabolics that are unmet by national and international pharmaceutical companies.

Our team

Our team has a habit of offering timely deliveries of excellent quality products. They work throughout the year to provide superior quality products to our valued clients and satisfy them.

They are always ready to perform different tasks, which helps us meet the diverse requirements of our clients. Our team practises a result-oriented work environment that shows their devotion towards the company.

Our corporate partners

To earn an eminent name in the industry, we are supported by trustworthy sources. Our partners provide us with supreme quality products that are efficient within a given time frame.

They use highly advanced manufacturing techniques and machinery in their WHO and ISO certified plants to maintain product quality, and have served the industry for a long time. We have trusted corporate partners.

Quality assurance

All our products are exceptional in quality and stringently tested by us on various industry parameters. We practise the latest techniques to process our product range, and our products are efficient in working and give a quick response.

They also provide endurance to the user. We offer our products in perfect packaging and deliver them in a committed time frame.

TIGRIS is a spirited organisation led by Mr. Tejpal Singh, with over 35 years in the pharmaceutical industry, bringing essential medicines and nutraceuticals to market since 2010.
